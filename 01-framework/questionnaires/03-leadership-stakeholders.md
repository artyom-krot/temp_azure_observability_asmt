# Workshop Questionnaire — Leadership & Business Stakeholders

**Audience:** Engineering managers, product owners, VP Engineering, CTO  
**Duration:** 45 min  
**Goal:** Understand business context, strategic goals, SLA commitments, and executive visibility needs

---

## Section 1 — Business Context & SLAs

1. What are the uptime/availability SLAs committed to consumers? Are they contractual?
2. Are SLAs differentiated by consumer tier or region?
3. How is SLA compliance currently measured and reported to consumers?
4. Have there been SLA breaches in the last 12 months? What were the consequences?
5. What is the business cost of a 1-hour P1 outage for a Tier 1 consumer?

---

## Section 2 — Strategic Observability Goals

6. How does the leadership team define "good observability"? What does success look like?
7. Is observability treated as a product feature, infrastructure concern, or shared platform capability?
8. What is the planned investment in observability over the next 12 months?
9. Are there regulatory or compliance requirements that observability must satisfy?
10. Is there a target maturity level or benchmark the organization is working toward?

---

## Section 3 — Visibility & Reporting

11. What observability data does leadership currently consume? (dashboards, reports, weekly summaries?)
12. Is there an executive dashboard showing platform health across all consumer environments?
13. How are major incidents communicated to leadership during and after the event?
14. What metrics would you want to see in a monthly platform health report?

---

## Section 4 — Team & Ownership

15. Who owns observability strategy today — is there a dedicated team, or is it distributed?
16. Is there a platform/SRE team, or do dev teams own their own observability?
17. Are observability skills and tooling considered a talent gap in the organization?
18. What is the relationship between the team that develops the monitoring setup and the teams that use it?

---

## Section 5 — Priorities & Constraints

19. What are the top 3 observability improvements you want to see from this assessment?
20. Are there any known constraints — budget, team size, technology choices that cannot change?
21. What does a successful outcome of this engagement look like to you in 6 months?
